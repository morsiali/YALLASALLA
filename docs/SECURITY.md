# Security, privacy and trust notes

This describes what the code **actually does**. It is not a compliance certification and makes no legal claims.

## 1. Data storage, access, retention and deletion

| Data                         | Where                                          | Who can read it                                                                                             | Retention                                                                                                      | Delete / export                                                                                                                           |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Account (phone, name, prefs) | `users`                                        | The user; support/admin (role-gated by RLS)                                                                 | Until deletion                                                                                                 | `POST /me/export`; `POST /me/delete` anonymises (phone replaced, name/email/prefs cleared, addresses/saved/devices/notifications removed) |
| Addresses                    | `addresses`; snapshot inside `orders.address`  | Owner; merchant of that order (snapshot only); support/admin                                                | Address book until deletion; order snapshot kept for the order record, **scrubbed on account deletion**        | Same                                                                                                                                      |
| Messages (text)              | `messages`                                     | The two parties; support/admin **only while an open report targets that conversation** (every read audited) | While the account is active; the user's message bodies are blanked on deletion                                 | Export includes own messages                                                                                                              |
| Photos / voice               | files in `STORAGE_DIR`, rows in `attachments`  | Participants via **signed, expiring URLs** (15 min) issued only after RLS lets them read the message        | Photos 365 days, voice 90 days (`retention_until`), purged by the maintenance job; deleted on account deletion | Export lists metadata                                                                                                                     |
| Transcripts                  | `transcriptions`                               | **Only the requesting user** (consent required first)                                                       | 30 days                                                                                                        | User can delete any time; withdrawing consent blocks new transcription immediately                                                        |
| Orders, payments, refunds    | `orders`, `order_items`, `payments`, `refunds` | Customer (own); merchant of the store; support/admin                                                        | Retained as financial records, without identifying details after deletion                                      | Export                                                                                                                                    |
| Loyalty ledger               | `loyalty_ledger` (append-only)                 | Owner; support/admin                                                                                        | Retained; expired points create `expire` rows                                                                  | Export                                                                                                                                    |
| OTP codes                    | `otp_codes` (HMAC hashes only)                 | Server only (no grant to the app role)                                                                      | 1 day                                                                                                          | —                                                                                                                                         |
| Consents                     | `consents` (history, never updated)            | Owner; support/admin                                                                                        | Retained as proof                                                                                              | Export                                                                                                                                    |
| Audit log                    | `audit_logs` (append-only)                     | Admin                                                                                                       | Retained                                                                                                       | —                                                                                                                                         |
| Analytics events             | `analytics_events`                             | Admin                                                                                                       | Retained; properties contain no PII (ids, counts, modes)                                                       | Included in the user export                                                                                                               |
| Logs                         | stdout                                         | Operators                                                                                                   | Your log platform                                                                                              | The request logger redacts `Authorization`; error logs record message/code only (no bodies, tokens, addresses, payment data)              |

Retention is enforced by `services/jobs.ts` (runs hourly in-process and via `POST /api/admin/jobs/run`).

## 2. Encryption: what is and is not claimed

- Messages and files are **protected in transit by TLS (you must terminate TLS in production) and at rest only to the extent your database/disk/bucket is encrypted**. They are **not end-to-end encrypted** — the server and authorised operators can read them. The product never says otherwise.
- No raw card data is ever accepted; the API takes a provider token only.

## 3. Threat model (summary)

| Threat                                          | Control in this repo                                                                                                                                                   | Residual risk / limitation                                                                                                                                   |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Cross-tenant or cross-user data access (IDOR)   | RLS on every table, column-limited grants, ownership checks, `NOT FOUND` (not 403) for foreign objects; tested directly against the DB                                 | Service-role code paths rely on explicit checks — covered by tests but a bug there bypasses RLS                                                              |
| Privilege escalation                            | Role read from DB per request; `users.role/status` not updatable by the app role; `business_guard`; admin/support cannot self-register                                 | Seed/admin provisioning is manual                                                                                                                            |
| OTP brute force / SMS pumping                   | 5 attempts per code (counter committed even when verification fails), 5-minute expiry, per-phone and per-IP rate limits                                                | In-memory limiter (single instance). Add CAPTCHA/risk checks and SMS-fraud controls with the live provider                                                   |
| Token theft (XSS)                               | Strict output encoding (React), no `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`, signed tokens (HMAC-SHA256) with expiry                                                                  | Bearer token is in `localStorage`. For production prefer an `HttpOnly; SameSite` cookie + CSRF protection, a strict CSP, and shorter token life with refresh |
| Client-side price/stock tampering               | Server reprices everything; ignores client totals; stock locked with `FOR UPDATE` and atomic decrement; DB `CHECK` on order totals                                     | —                                                                                                                                                            |
| Duplicate / replayed orders, payments, webhooks | Idempotency keys, unique constraints, HMAC webhook verification with constant-time compare, event-id table                                                             | Provider-specific replay windows must be added with the live PSP                                                                                             |
| Malicious uploads                               | Allow-list of MIME types, magic-byte sniffing, size limits (5 MB / 3 MB voice), random storage keys, `nosniff`, signed expiring URLs, path-traversal-safe storage      | No malware scanning; add it before accepting public traffic                                                                                                  |
| Abuse: spam, harassment, fake engagement        | Message/upload/order rate limits, block + report, moderation workflow, loyalty earns only on delivered orders with a daily cap (messages/reviews/referrals never earn) | No ML spam detection; duplicate-account detection is limited to unique phone numbers                                                                         |
| Tampering with audit/financial history          | `audit_logs`, `loyalty_ledger`, `order_status_history` are append-only by trigger (even for the service role); DB-enforced order state machine                         | A superuser/owner can still alter triggers — protect and monitor owner credentials                                                                           |
| Secrets in source or client                     | `.env.example` placeholders only; production start-up rejects dev secrets; client receives no secrets; provider status exposes booleans only                           | Rotate secrets on any suspicion; use a secret manager                                                                                                        |
| Injection                                       | Parameterised SQL everywhere, `zod` validation on every body/query, role/identity never taken from input                                                               | —                                                                                                                                                            |
| Denial of service                               | Body size limit, per-route rate limits                                                                                                                                 | Needs edge/WAF protection and shared limiter for production                                                                                                  |
| Sensitive data in logs                          | Redaction + no body logging                                                                                                                                            | Verify your log pipeline                                                                                                                                     |

## 4. Known limitations (be aware before launch)

1. Auth tokens are bearer tokens in `localStorage` (see above); no refresh-token rotation or device list.
2. Rate limiting and the SSE bus are in-process.
3. No malware scanning, no content moderation automation, no fraud scoring.
4. Allergen/dietary tags are merchant-declared; they are not verified. Do not market the product as medically safe for allergies without a verification process.
5. Order writes use a privileged role with application-level authorization (tested), unlike reads which are enforced by RLS.
6. `localStorage` stores the basket draft and checkout draft on the device (no card data).
7. Account recovery without the phone number requires manual support; there is no identity-proofing flow.

## 5. Needs qualified legal / compliance review before any production launch

- UAE federal data-protection law (PDPL) and any emirate/free-zone rules: lawful basis, notices, consent wording, cross-border transfers (hosting location, SMS/AI/STT vendors), retention periods, DSAR handling, breach notification.
- Consumer-protection and e-commerce rules: price display, refund/cancellation rights, delivery promises, terms of sale, invoices/VAT (5 %) treatment, marketplace-operator liability for merchant-supplied information.
- Payments regulation and PSP onboarding (card data scope, stored-value/loyalty-points treatment, refunds).
- Food-safety and halal-labelling responsibilities of merchants vs. the platform; alcohol/pork/restricted-goods policies.
- Voice recording and transcription consent wording, retention and any employee/merchant access rules.
- Terms of use and privacy notice: the texts in the app are **placeholders**.
- Platform store policies (Apple/Google) for account deletion, data-safety declarations and payments.
